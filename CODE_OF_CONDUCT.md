# Code of Conduct

Tusk's Vault is a small project maintained by one person. This is short on
purpose — a four-tier enforcement ladder would be describing a community that
does not exist yet.

**The standard.** Be decent. Assume good faith, keep criticism about the code,
and take a "no" without relitigating it. Harassment, personal attacks, and
demeaning comments about who someone is are not welcome here, in issues, pull
requests, or anywhere else this project is discussed.

**Reporting.** The project's only private channel today is
[GitHub's private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/KochiTusker/Tusks-Vault/security/advisories/new)
— open the **Security** tab and click *Report a vulnerability*. Lead with
`[conduct]`. Conduct reports are welcome through it, they are treated
confidentially, and you do not have to raise anything in public first.

**What happens then.** The maintainer reads it and decides — a word, an edit, or
removing someone from the project's spaces. There is no committee to appeal to;
that is the honest shape of a project this size.

If a community Discord launches ([roadmap](ROADMAP.md)), this page gets the
moderation route that comes with it.
