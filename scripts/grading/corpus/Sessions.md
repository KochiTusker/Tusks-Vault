# Session log

## Session 12 — The Ninefold Rest

The party found Ser Alric drinking alone at the Ninefold Rest in Kelmoor. He
would not discuss Dunmar. Maera the Ashbound recognised him and left before he
saw her.

The party learned that the Ashen Court has posted a bounty on Maera. The bounty
is **four hundred crowns**.

## Session 13 — Salvage

The party hired a salvage boat into the drowned quarter of Dunmar and recovered
a sealed Vane strongbox.

The strongbox has not been opened. Nobody has established what is inside it.

## Session 14 — The Road to Kelmoor

The party travelled from the estuary to Kelmoor. The journey took nine days.
Nothing of consequence happened on the road, which the party found suspicious.
