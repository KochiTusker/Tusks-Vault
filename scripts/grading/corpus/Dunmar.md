# Dunmar

Dunmar was a walled port city on the Vell Estuary. It fell in the **Third Age**,
on the night the Ashen Gate was breached, when the sea took the lower quarter
within a single day.

The city had four gates. Only the Ashen Gate, on the eastern wall, was breached;
the Salt Gate, the Rook Gate and the Chandler's Gate all held until the water
made them irrelevant.

Dunmar has never been resettled. Salvagers work the shallows in summer. Nobody
works them in winter, because the winter tides run through the drowned quarter
hard enough to pull a boat under.

The city was held by House Vane for six generations before the fall.

## The Vell Estuary

The Vell is the river Dunmar was built on. It is not to be confused with the
Vellin, a smaller river two hundred miles north with no city on it at all.
