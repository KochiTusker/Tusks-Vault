# The Houses of the Estuary

## House Vane

House Vane held Dunmar for six generations. Its banner is a **grey heron on a
black field**. After the fall the house is reduced to a single surviving
member, Ser Alric Vane, and a claim nobody honours.

Ser Alric Vane is also called the Grey Knight. He drinks at the Ninefold Rest
in Kelmoor and refuses to speak of the siege. He believes his brother Corwin
died at the Ashen Gate.

## House Vayne

House Vayne is a different house entirely, of the inland hills, and is spelled
with a Y. Its banner is a **red stag on white**. The two houses are not related
and the confusion is a long-running joke in the estuary towns.

## House Corrin

House Corrin holds Kelmoor. Its banner is a **nine-flamed hearth**, for the inn
that made the town's name. Lady Ismet Corrin has ruled it for eleven years.
