# The party

Four names appear in the session logs from Session 09 onward.

## Roswyn Kett

A salvager out of Dunmar before the party found her, and the only one of the
four who has ever crewed a boat. She held a salvage licence for eleven years
and lost it after an argument with the harbourmaster at Kelmoor, which ended
with the harbourmaster in the water.

She has been barred from the Ninefold Rest twice and readmitted twice. Lady
Ismet Corrin has described her, in writing, as "an asset best kept at sea".

## Bryn Tallow

A hedge-priest of the Nine Flames and the party's healer. Bryn has never drawn
a weapon in any session recorded here. He reads the old script, which nobody
else in the party can.

He cannot swim, and has twice refused to board a boat — once at Dunmar, once
at the Kelmoor quay. He gets sick on still water.

## Odo Marrow

A fence and a negotiator. Odo has talked the party out of three fights and
into one. He keeps the money, and the party has never asked to see the ledger.

He is a poor sleeper and takes the last watch by choice. He has never held a
rank of any kind and says he does not intend to.

## Vesper Quill

A scholar of the drowned quarter, and the party's mapmaker. Vesper is
formally correct with strangers to the point that two innkeepers have assumed
an insult where none was meant.

Vesper has read every account of the fall of Dunmar that survives, and
corrects people who have not — including, on one occasion, Ser Alric Vane.
